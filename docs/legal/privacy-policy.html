Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tape (“Tape,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Tape mobile application and related services (the “Service”).

Tape is a recruiting and highlight-sharing platform for athletes, college and program recruiters, and brands. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Service.

Contact: support@watchtape.app

1. Who can use Tape

You must be at least 13 years old to create a Tape account. We ask for your date of birth at signup and reject accounts below this age.

If you are under 18, you are considered a minor on Tape. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete that account and its data. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact support@watchtape.app.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

Account information

  • Email address and password (passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; Tape never stores your raw password)
  • Display name
  • Date of birth
  • Account role (Athlete, Recruiter, or Brand)

Profile information

Depending on your role, you may choose to provide:

  • Profile photo
  • High school or organization, graduation year, job title
  • Sport, position, and home state
  • Athletic measurables: height, weight, forty-yard dash time
  • GPA
  • Target schools
  • Social media handles (Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat)

Athletic and academic details are optional. Anything you add to your profile is visible to other Tape users.

Content you upload

  • Video clips and their generated thumbnail images
  • Captions and tags
  • Direct messages you send to other users

Safety reports

  • Reports you submit about content, users, or messages, including the reason and any details you provide
  • Records of users you block

2.2 Information generated by using the Service

  • Videos you save (bookmarks) and athletes you save to shortlists or scouting boards
  • Accounts you follow
  • Records of profiles you view, and records of users who view your profile
  • Video view counts
  • Message read status
  • Subscription status (whether you have an active Tape Pro subscription)
  • Account creation date and content timestamps

2.3 Information we do not collect

Tape does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics or tracking software. We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or device advertising identifier, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and maintain your account and authenticate you
  • Display your profile and content to other users
  • Operate the video feed, search, and recruiting features
  • Deliver direct messages between recruiters, brands, and athletes
  • Show athletes who has viewed their profile (a Tape Pro feature)
  • Count video views and profile views
  • Process and maintain Tape Pro subscriptions
  • Enforce free-tier limits, such as the monthly direct message cap
  • Review reports, enforce blocks, and remove content or accounts that violate our Terms of Service
  • Diagnose problems and improve the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations

5. How information is shared

With other users. Your profile, uploaded videos, captions, tags, social handles, and view counts are visible to other Tape users. Direct messages are visible to the other participant in the conversation. Athletes on Tape Pro can see which users viewed their profile.

With service providers. We use the following third parties to operate Tape:

These providers process data on our behalf and are subject to their own privacy policies. Tape never receives your full payment card details; Apple handles all subscription billing.

For legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information if we believe it is reasonably necessary to comply with a law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request; to enforce our Terms of Service; or to protect the safety, rights, or property of our users or the public.

In a business transfer. If Tape is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Content visibility and minors

Tape is a public-facing recruiting platform. Content that athletes post is intended to be discoverable by recruiters and brands.

Because many athletes on Tape are minors, we apply additional safeguards:

  • Athletes cannot start direct message conversations; only recruiters and brands can initiate contact
  • Every user can report content and block any other user
  • Blocking is mutual in effect: blocked users are hidden from your feed, your search results, and your inbox, and neither party can message the other

Parents or guardians of a user under 18 may contact support@watchtape.app to request review or deletion of their child’s account.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active.

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, videos, bookmarks, conversations and messages, profile view records, scouting boards, saved athletes, subscription record, follows, and block and report records, along with your Firebase Authentication user.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Some information may persist temporarily in backups or where retention is required by law. Note that cancelling a Tape Pro subscription is handled through your Apple ID and is separate from deleting your account.

8. Security

We use industry-standard protections including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all data in transit, token-based authentication, and server-side authorization that prevents users from accessing or modifying data they do not own. Passwords are managed by Google Firebase Authentication and are never stored by Tape.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information (most fields are editable in Edit Profile)
  • Delete your account and associated data (Settings → Delete Account)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive a copy of your information in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact support@watchtape.app. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

California residents. Under the CCPA/CPRA you have the rights described above and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. Tape does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

10. International users

Tape is operated from the United States, and your information is stored and processed in the United States. If you use the Service from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. If we make material changes, we will provide additional notice, such as in the app. Continuing to use Tape after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
